# Model sources

Downloaded from NASA Science 3D Resources, 14 November 2026.

| Local asset | Source / credit |
| --- | --- |
| `viking1.glb` | [Viking Lander](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/viking-lander/) — NASA / Michael D. Carbajal |
| `opportunity.glb` | [Mars Exploration Rover – Opportunity (MER-B)](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars-exploration-rover-opportunity-mer-b/) — NASA / Ames Research Center |

Models retain their source materials. Runtime transforms center them and scale their visible heights for this game. They are not AI-generated.

See the [NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). This game is not endorsed by NASA.

Phoenix remains a stylized procedural representation. No suitable official model of its deployed surface configuration was verified; the EDL capsule model is not used as a substitute.

The local Draco decoder under `public/draco` comes from the installed Three.js distribution, whose decoder is distributed under the Apache 2.0 license. It is hosted locally so compressed models do not depend on a decoder CDN.
