## 2. Science rule check

- ✅ The game never says Viking found life. Viking says "no proof"; the case board says "nobody knows yet".
- ✅ The game never says Mars has rivers today. Water means ancient liquid water plus ice today.
- ✅ The game says water once soaked the rocks, not that lakes covered the surface (see O7).
- ✅ The virtual-simulation note on the main menu is correct: the real landing sites are thousands of kilometres apart, and each machine's true site appears in the Journal.

## 3. Source conflicts (dates left out of the game on purpose)

Where two official NASA pages disagree on a small detail, the game avoids the detail instead of choosing a side.

| Topic | Page A | Page B | What the game says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Viking 1 last contact | NASA Science: 11 Nov 1982 | NSSDCA: 13 Nov 1982 | "November 1982" |
| Phoenix trench enlarged | JPL: 15 June (sol 20) | Photojournal: 12 June 2008 (sol 18) | "four days later" |
| Opportunity launch | JPL and NASA: 7 Jul 2003 (US time) | NSSDCA: 8 Jul 2003 (UT) | "7 Jul 2003" (US time) |
| Opportunity landing | NSSDCA: 25 Jan 2004 (UT) | US Pacific time: 24 Jan 2004 | "25 Jan 2004" (UT) |
| Why Phoenix went silent | NSSDCA: power ran out as summer ended | Photojournal: panels stopped in the dark Martian winter | "the Sun sank lower and power dropped" |
